Our story

Built from a love of good coffee and unhurried mornings, Northlight is a neighborhood café where the details matter.

Thoughtful, from bean to cup

We source seasonal beans, steam silky milk with care, and bake small batches throughout the day so the room always smells like something welcoming. Our menu balances the familiar and the playful: classic cappuccinos, maple-forward specials, and gentle herbal teas for slower afternoons.

A place to settle in

  • ✦Soft seating, warm woods, and plenty of natural light.
  • ✦Quiet corners for remote work and easy conversation.
  • ✦Friendly staff who remember your favorite order.
